# Trigger context

An investment opportunity payment can warrant review when it is large, directed to a first-time payee, or follows a recent inbound credit that is inconsistent with known activity.

# Evidence requirements

Capture the alerted transaction, account baseline, related funds movement, payment rail, and any documented explanation before recommending human escalation.
