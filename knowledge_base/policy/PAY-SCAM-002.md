# Evidence quality

Material claims in an investigation draft must cite a source transaction, account attribute, or approved procedure. Where evidence is incomplete, the draft must state the limitation and recommend human review or further investigation.

# Analyst review

An authorised analyst reviews every draft and can approve, edit, request further evidence, record insufficient evidence, or escalate under the bank's established procedures. The analyst's decision and rationale form the official case outcome.

# Data handling

Use the minimum data necessary for the investigation. Do not include direct identifiers, account numbers, addresses, or other unnecessary personal information in model prompts, evaluation data, or application logs.
