# Trigger context

Multiple unrelated inbound credits followed by a rapid onward transfer may be a mule-account indicator. Review timing, counterparties, amount variance, and the customer's normal account pattern.

# Limitation

This indicator is not a fraud conclusion. Missing transaction context must reduce confidence and be visible in the analyst brief.
