# Trigger context

Remote-access support, impersonation, or urgent technical-support payment descriptions require a documented review of payment amount, payee novelty, channel, and available customer context.

# Outcome boundary

The copilot may assemble evidence and cite procedure. An authorised analyst decides whether any customer communication or operational action is appropriate.
