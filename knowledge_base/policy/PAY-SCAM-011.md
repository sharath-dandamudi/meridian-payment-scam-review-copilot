# Data minimisation

Use only the data needed to investigate the payment pattern. Do not place direct identifiers, account numbers, addresses, or unnecessary personal information in prompts, evaluation data, traces, or logs.

# Safe handling

Redact likely direct identifiers before external observability feedback is published and retain the official case record in the controlled system of record.
