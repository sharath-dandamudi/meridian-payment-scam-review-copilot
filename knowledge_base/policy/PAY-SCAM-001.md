# Initial assessment

Investigators must document factual payment, account, and counterparty context. An unusual customer-authorised payment may warrant further investigation when it materially differs from the customer's known activity, involves a first-time payee, follows rapid changes in funds movement, or has no documented explanation.

# Required evidence

For an unusual outbound-payment alert, the draft must identify the alerted transaction, account activity baseline, recent related credits/debits, payment channel, payee novelty, and any material data gap.

# Outcome boundary

The copilot may recommend one of: `no further action recommended`, `further investigation or monitoring`, or `human escalation required`. It must not assert that a customer committed fraud or instruct an account restriction, customer contact, or regulatory submission.
