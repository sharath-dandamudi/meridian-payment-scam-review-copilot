# Trigger context

Review an unusual PayID or first-time-payee payment against the customer's normal outbound-payment range, channel history, payee novelty, and stated purpose.

# Review standard

Record factual indicators only. A new payee or large payment is an investigation signal, not proof of a scam. Refer uncertain cases to an authorised analyst.
