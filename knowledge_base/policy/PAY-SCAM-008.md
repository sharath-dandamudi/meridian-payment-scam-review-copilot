# Contextual explanations

Education fees, a documented vehicle purchase, relocation, or other documented purposes may explain an unusual customer-authorised payment. When a first-time payee is involved, compare the payment with usual activity and record the explanation as context, not verification.

# Review standard

Where context reduces concern but evidence remains incomplete, recommend further investigation or monitoring and retain human review.
