# Trigger context

Review a new BSB/account recipient or changed supplier invoice when the payment materially exceeds the known outbound-payment baseline or differs from established recipient behaviour.

# Review standard

Clearly distinguish a payment-pattern anomaly from a confirmed invoice scam. Record available evidence and route the brief to an authorised analyst.
