# Required evidence

For an unusual outbound payment, record the payment rail, channel, transaction time, amount, first-time-payee status, and whether the payment differs from the account baseline.

# Data quality

If a source is unavailable, record the gap. Do not infer account facts or substitute generated content for missing evidence.
