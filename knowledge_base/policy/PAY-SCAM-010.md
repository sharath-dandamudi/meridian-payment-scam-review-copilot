# Analyst review

Every copilot brief requires an authorised analyst to approve, edit, seek more evidence, record insufficient evidence, or escalate under established procedures.

# Case record

The final case outcome is the analyst decision and rationale. The copilot draft, tool evidence, policy citations, gates, and trace are supporting records only.
